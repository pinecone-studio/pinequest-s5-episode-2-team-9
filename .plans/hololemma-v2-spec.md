# Hololemma v2: Spec (user decisions, 2026-10-27)

After testing v1 ("so nice"), the user asked for more models and more realism. Build in this order, deploying each:

1. **Human brain** ✅: HRA brain (Allen structures), CC BY 4.0, 22 parts.
2. **Skeleton** and **muscles** as two separate models:
   - Source: BodyParts3D (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP), the basis of Z-Anatomy. The user accepted ShareAlike for the derived model files.
   - Student-level groups: ~40 skeleton parts and ~40 major muscles.
3. **Engines:**
   - **Rocket engine** (liquid-fuel: tanks, turbopump, injector, combustion chamber, bell nozzle).
   - **Electric motor** (stator, rotor, magnets, coils, commutator/brushes or BLDC).
4. **Atoms / quantum physics**, all four:
   - **Build atoms:** protons, neutrons and electrons give the element, isotope or ion, its stability, and its place in the periodic table.
   - **Real orbitals:** hydrogen wavefunction probability clouds for n, l and m.
   - **Energy levels and light:** excite an electron; it emits a photon of the correct colour (hydrogen spectrum).
   - **Quantum experiments:** double-slit interference, wave–particle duality, spin.
5. **More realism**, for the heart, robot dog and engines (applies to all models):
   - A **Realistic view** switch: PBR materials and lighting, with the hologram as the other view.
   - **More detail** in the meshes and parts.
   - **Optional motion:** heart tempo with blood flow and valves; dog gait; engines running.
